# Direct care (membership) agreements must allow termination and refund unearned fees

**Rule.** A direct medical care agreement is not insurance or a discount medical plan, but it must let either party end it on written notice, treat a periodic fee as earned only when its month is complete, and refund all unearned fees when the patient ends it.

**Exact text.**

> "A direct medical care agreement is not insurance and is not subject to regulation by the department of commerce and insurance." (T.C.A. § 63-1-504(a) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "Provide that, upon termination of this agreement by the individual patient, all unearned fees are to be returned to the patient." (T.C.A. § 63-1-504(e)(3) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Office checklist.**

- Check the membership contract for the 3 required terms in § 63-1-504(e): written-notice termination by either party; fees earned only after the paid month ends; refund of unearned fees on patient termination.
- Do not call the plan insurance or a discount medical plan in marketing (§ 63-1-504(a), (d)).

**Records to keep.**

Keep each signed agreement and termination notice with the refund record.

**Open.** Whether a dental membership plan meets the chapter's definition of a "direct medical care agreement" (§ 63-1-502) is not transcribed here — check the source.
