# Do not fire an employee for refusing to take part in, or stay silent about, illegal activities

**Rule.** An employer must not discharge an employee solely for refusing to participate in, or refusing to remain silent about, illegal activities, which include violations of state or federal criminal or civil law and regulations protecting public health, safety or welfare (T.C.A. § 50-1-304).

**Exact text.**

> "No employee shall be discharged or terminated solely for refusing to participate in, or for refusing to remain silent about, illegal activities." (T.C.A. § 50-1-304(b) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Office checklist.**

- Route any staff report of billing, infection-control or scope-of-practice violations to the owner dentist in writing, and document the response.
- Before terminating someone who recently raised such a concern, have the owner document the independent reasons.

**Records to keep.**

Keep the concern, the response and any termination file.
