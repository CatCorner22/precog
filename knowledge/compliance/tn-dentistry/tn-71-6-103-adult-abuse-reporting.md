# Report suspected abuse, neglect or exploitation of an adult immediately

**Rule.** Any person with reasonable cause to suspect that an adult has suffered abuse, neglect or exploitation must report it, orally or in writing, immediately to the Department (Adult Protective Services).

**Exact text.**

> "Any person, including, but not limited to, a physician, nurse, social worker, department personnel, coroner, medical examiner, alternate care facility employee, or caretaker, having reasonable cause to suspect that an adult has suffered abuse, neglect, or exploitation, shall report or cause reports to be made in accordance with this part." (T.C.A. § 71-6-103(b)(1) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "An oral or written report shall be made immediately to the department upon knowledge of the occurrence of suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of an adult." (T.C.A. § 71-6-103(c) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Standard: reasonable cause to suspect (§ 71-6-103(b)(1)).
- Timing: immediately, orally or in writing (§ 71-6-103(c)).
- The adult's death does not end the duty to report the circumstances (§ 71-6-103(b)(1)).

**Office checklist.**

- Give the report the information § 71-6-103(c) lists, if known: the adult's name and address, the caregiver, the adult's age, and the nature and extent of the abuse, neglect or exploitation.
- Watch for financial exploitation signs at the front desk (for example, a caregiver who pays with the patient's card and blocks the patient from talking to staff).
