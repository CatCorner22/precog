# Report suspected child abuse or neglect immediately

**Rule.** Any person who knows of, or is called on to aid, a child with an injury or condition that reasonably indicates brutality, abuse or neglect must report it immediately; the duty is personal and covers everyone on the dental team.

**Exact text.**

> "Any person who has knowledge of or is called upon to render aid to any child who is suffering from or has sustained any wound, injury, disability, or physical or mental condition shall report such harm immediately if the harm is of such a nature as to reasonably indicate that it has been caused by brutality, abuse or neglect" (T.C.A. § 37-1-403(a)(1) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Timing: immediately (T.C.A. § 37-1-403(a)(1)).
- Standard: the harm reasonably indicates, or on available information reasonably appears to have been caused by, brutality, abuse or neglect (§ 37-1-403(a)(1)).

**Office checklist.**

- Train every staff member that the duty is personal: telling the dentist does not by itself satisfy it.
- Keep the reporting contact for the Tennessee Department of Children's Services posted where clinical staff can see it.
- Chart objective findings (location, size, description of injuries, what the child or caregiver said) without diagnosing abuse.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source; keep the chart note under the dental records rule (card tn-0460-02-12-dental-records).

**Where to report.** By telephone or otherwise to the juvenile judge for the child, the Department of Children's Services (local office or centralized intake), the sheriff of the child's county, or the chief municipal law enforcement official (T.C.A. § 37-1-403(a)(2)).

**Open.** None.
