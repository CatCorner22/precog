# Office air quality - nitrous oxide and mercury vapor limits (PPM)

**Rule.** OSHA sets no permissible exposure limit (PEL) for nitrous oxide. NIOSH recommends a limit of 25 ppm for waste anesthetic gas. A NIOSH limit is a recommendation, not a law. For mercury vapor, OSHA Table Z-2 sets a limit of 1 mg/10m³ (0.1 mg/m³).

**Exact text.**
> "NIOSH REL TWA 25 ppm (46 mg/m3) (TWA over the time exposed) [*Note: REL for exposure to waste anesthetic gas.] ... OSHA PEL none" (NIOSH Pocket Guide, Nitrous oxide).
> "Mercury (Z37.8-1971) 1 mg/10m3" (29 CFR 1910.1000, Table Z-2).

**Thresholds.**
- Nitrous oxide: 25 ppm time-weighted average during administration (NIOSH recommendation).
- Mercury vapor: 0.1 mg/m³ (OSHA Table Z-2). NIOSH recommends 0.05 mg/m³ time-weighted average and a 0.1 mg/m³ ceiling.

**Office checklist.**
- Use a scavenging system with nitrous oxide, and keep a record of its maintenance.
- Store and handle amalgam waste so that vapor stays contained. See the amalgam and waste cards.
