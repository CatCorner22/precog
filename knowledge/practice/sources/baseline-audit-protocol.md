# Phase 0 baseline audit protocol

- Source/Author-Origin: Blake Reagan research package
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Ingested: 2026-11-19
- Tags: baseline, audit, evidence, interviews, risk register

## Summary

Provides a ten-business-day process for establishing a documented operating
baseline, testing evidence, interviewing roles, and loading verified findings
into a risk register.

## Key concepts

- Privilege-sensitive legal work belongs with counsel; operational evidence is
  not a substitute for legal advice.
- Every pass/fail item needs an evidence artifact or a recorded reason it is
  unavailable.
- A report is a lead until corroborated; false alarms should be closed with
  evidence rather than retained as open findings.
- Triage follows verification and uses the separate prioritization rubric.

## Notable data

- The audit is scoped for ten business days across document review, interviews,
  credential checks, financial walkthroughs, and safety/system checks.
- Deliverables include an evidence-linked worksheet, risk register, and a
  concise leadership evidence pack.

## Relation to existing knowledge

Adds an operations-first audit sequence; Tennessee and federal obligations are
not restated in this source note or the Practice reference.
