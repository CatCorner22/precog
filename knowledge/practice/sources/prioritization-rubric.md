# Prioritization rubric

- Source/Author-Origin: Blake Reagan research package
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Ingested: 2026-11-19
- Tags: prioritization, risk register, hard gates, scoring, remediation

## Summary

Defines a repeatable order for validating and remediating operational findings.
It separates immediate override conditions from an additive score and assigns
deadlines and review cadence by tier.

## Key concepts

- Four binary gates are evaluated before scoring.
- Six weighted inputs score findings that pass all gates.
- Credit for a compensating control requires a named independent actor,
  appropriate cadence, and verifiable artifact.
- Owners, interim controls, due dates, and evidence belong in a dated register.

## Notable data

- Tier 1: interim control within 7 calendar days and permanent fix within 30.
- Score inputs include impact, likelihood, legal, detection, recoverability,
  compensating-control credit, and aggregation.
- Tier bands and scoring anchors are reproduced in the reference.

## Relation to existing knowledge

Adds a generic operations prioritization workflow beside the separate
Compliance desk. It does not replace legal review or existing ControlGuard.
