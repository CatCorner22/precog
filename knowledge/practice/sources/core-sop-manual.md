# Core SOP manual

- Source/Author-Origin: Blake Reagan research package
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Ingested: 2026-11-19
- Tags: SOP index, roles, cadence, evidence, process improvement

## Summary

Provides role-owned operating procedures for governance, access, clinical
work, safety, records, finance, staffing, patient experience, facilities, and
continuous improvement.

## Key concepts

- Each process has an owner, review cadence, steps, exceptions, and evidence.
- Controls separate preparation, approval, custody, and reconciliation.
- Metrics pair a target with a source record and a review cadence.
- Patient feedback is balanced with operational outcomes and privacy safeguards.

## Notable data

- The manual contains 14 process documents indexed in §40 of the reference.
- Examples of source targets include 100% role-access review, 95% delay
  notifications, and 95% first meaningful complaint responses within two
  business days.

## Relation to existing knowledge

Makes existing Precog control principles operational at the SOP level. Legal
requirements remain in the Compliance desk and are not re-explained here.
