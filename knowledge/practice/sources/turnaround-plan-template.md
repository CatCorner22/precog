# Turnaround plan template

- Source/Author-Origin: Blake Reagan research package
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Ingested: 2026-11-19
- Tags: functions, operating model, dashboard, turnaround

## Summary

Lists 17 operating functions and their roles, common failure modes, core SOPs,
controls, and health metrics. It also describes a baseline-to-sustainment
turnaround sequence.

## Key concepts

- Every function has a named owner, metric, and evidence artifact.
- A person may hold more than one function, but accountability remains explicit.
- Findings move through baseline, immediate containment, standards,
  improvement, and sustainment phases.

## Notable data

- The 17 functions are the sections [10] through [26] of the reference.
- The source sets target values for chart audits, recall, template fill,
  collections, records requests, training, safety checks, and coverage.
- The steady-state dashboard groups people, money, clinical, safety/compliance,
  and growth measures.

## Relation to existing knowledge

Supplies a practical operating map that complements the Compliance desk without
copying Tennessee or federal legal explanations.
