# Patient survey and retention guide

- Source/Author-Origin: Blake Reagan research package
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Ingested: 2026-11-19
- Tags: patient experience, survey, retention, service recovery

## Summary

Defines a neutral patient survey, transparent response-rate reporting,
complaint-recovery steps, and cohort measures for actual return and referral
completion.

## Key concepts

- Survey items measure distinct domains; do not collapse unlike measures into
  one score.
- Keep denominators, response rates, periods, channels, and case mix visible.
- Use confidential feedback without filtering for positive sentiment.
- Compare stated intent with actual return, reactivation, and referral completion.

## Notable data

- The source provides 18 patient-facing items, reproduced in §60 where generic.
- Response rate is completed responses divided by eligible invitations.
- Published percentages are research findings, not local performance targets.

## Relation to existing knowledge

Adds patient voice and service recovery to existing operational metrics. It
does not guarantee retention outcomes or replace clinical judgment.
