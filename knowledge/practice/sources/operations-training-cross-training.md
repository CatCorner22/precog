# Operations training and cross-training

- Source/Author-Origin: Blake Reagan research package
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Ingested: 2026-11-19
- Tags: training, competency, cross-training, continuity, succession

## Summary

Describes role-based competency standards, a recurring training tracker, and a
tested backup model for critical work.

## Key concepts

- Define recurring work by action, quality bar, time bar, and evidence.
- Track requirements by person and role, with completion dates and artifacts.
- A backup counts only after performing the work, not from a written assignment.
- Keep one-page runbooks and test the backup against actual work.

## Notable data

- Each critical function has at least two capable people at different locations.
- A backup must have performed the function within the previous six months.
- Cross-location backup practice occurs quarterly; runbooks are tested annually.
- The tracker is checked monthly, with 60/30/7-day expiry alerts.

## Relation to existing knowledge

Adds continuity and competence practices to Precog's existing controls and
succession concepts; legal training requirements remain in the Compliance desk.
