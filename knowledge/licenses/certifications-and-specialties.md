# Certifications and specialties — how each is earned

**Rule.** Extra duties for hygienists and registered assistants start only after the Board issues the certification, and a dentist may hold a specialty certification only in a Board-recognized branch (0460-02-.06; 0460-03-.06; 0460-03-.12; 0460-04-.04, .05, .09, .11).

## Dental specialties the Board certifies — 0460-02-.06(1)
Dental anesthesiology; dental public health; endodontics; oral and maxillofacial radiology; oral and maxillofacial surgery; oral and maxillofacial pathology; oral medicine; orofacial pain; orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics; pediatric dentistry (pedodontics); periodontics; prosthodontics.

**How.** A Tennessee dental license (with the 0460-02-.02 exception), a specialty application and fee, and either completion of the specialty training the rule lists for that specialty or certification by the American Board of that specialty, confirmed by letter sent directly from that board's secretary; accepting American Board certification is at the Board's discretion (0460-02-.06(2)).

## Cautions
- Even with restorative or prosthetic certification, no hygienist or assistant may diagnose the need for a restoration or prosthesis, prepare or cut tooth or soft tissue, modify existing structure, remove caries, bases or liners, establish vertical dimension of occlusion or interocclusal records, deliver or adjust an appliance, or use a high-speed handpiece intraorally. (0460-03-.10(5); 0460-04-.10(5))
- Restorative/prosthetic certification expires or retires with the underlying license or registration, and the current renewal certificate must be displayed at work. (0460-03-.10(2)-(3); 0460-04-.10(2)-(3))
- "Once eligible for certification, the registered dental assistant shall not monitor nitrous oxide until certification has been issued by the Board." (0460-04-.05(2))
- A hygienist's nitrous monitoring certificate lapses when the hygiene license expires or is retired, and the current renewal certificate must be displayed at work. (0460-03-.06(2)(c)-(d))
- A hygienist with only a monitoring certificate may not administer nitrous oxide until completing the administration-and-monitoring course and receiving Board certification. (0460-03-.06(2)(a))
