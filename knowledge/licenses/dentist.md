# Licensed dentist — what Tennessee allows

**Rule.** Dentistry is the evaluation, diagnosis, prevention and treatment of conditions of the oral cavity, maxillofacial area and associated structures, "provided by a dentist within the scope of such dentist's education, training, and experience, in accordance with the ethics of the profession and applicable law" (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(a)).

## Acts that count as practicing dentistry — T.C.A. § 63-5-108(b)
Diagnosing, prescribing for or treating oral and maxillofacial conditions (b)(1); extracting teeth (b)(2); repairing or filling cavities (b)(3); correcting malformations of teeth or jaws (b)(4); oral and maxillofacial surgery (b)(5); impressions leading to replacement teeth or cosmetic metal apparatus, subject to (d) (b)(6); making or repairing dentures, bridges, crowns or appliances except on a dentist's written work order (b)(7); placing or adjusting them (b)(8); interpreting dental radiographs (b)(11); administering anesthetic other than topical, except that a dentist may delegate local anesthetic to a hygienist with a Board permit (b)(12); using the titles "Dentist", "Dental Surgeon", "Oral Surgeon", "Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon", "D.D.S." or "D.M.D." (b)(13); operating a place where dental services are performed (b)(15); and authorizing teledentistry (b)(16).

## Delegating to staff
- The dentist delegates to assistants only procedures they are adequately trained for, under the dentist's "direct supervision and full responsibility" (0460-04-.08(1)).
- Direct supervision means the dentist is continuously present within the physical confines of the office (T.C.A. § 63-5-115(b)(1)).
- No more than 3 hygienists under general supervision at once (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(f)(1)).
- Local anesthesia by a certified hygienist: the dentist is physically present at the same office location (0460-03-.09(5)).

## Licensure — 0460-02-.01
Board application with all documents filed together; certificate of graduation and transcript sent directly from a CODA-accredited dental school (no license until the degree is conferred); a passport-style photo from the last 12 months; 2 character letters from dental professionals; proof of citizenship or lawful presence; fees; and disclosure of convictions, out-of-state licensure denial or discipline, loss of hospital privileges, and certain civil judgments or settlements.

## Ethics and conduct
Board ethics rule 0460-01-.13 and unprofessional-conduct rule 0460-01-.12 (see the Compliance desk cards). The ADA Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct is a professional standard, not Tennessee law.

## Cautions
- Ownership: operating a dental office is itself the practice of dentistry (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(b)(15)); see card tn-63-5-121-dental-practice-ownership.
- Teledentistry must be consistent with in-person care (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(b)(16)); see card tn-0460-01-19-teledentistry.
- Staff who work beyond scope expose the dentist and the staff member to discipline (T.C.A. § 63-5-116).
