# Practical dental assistant — what Tennessee allows

**Rule.** A practical dental assistant is a dentist's auxiliary employee receiving chairside training from a licensed dentist, or a student in a CODA-accredited program; they work only under the direct supervision of a licensed dentist (0460-04-.01(1)).

## Allowed when delegated — direct supervision
The same delegable list as a registered assistant in 0460-04-.08(3) (processing radiographs, topical fluoride, vitals and charting, infection control, matrices, temporaries, rubber dam, dressings, orthodontic wire and bracket tasks, retraction cord, supragingival cement removal by hand instruments, topical anesthetic, pulp testing, and so on). See the registered dental assistant table, "Allowed when delegated".

## Not allowed
- Everything in 0460-04-.08(4), including administering any anesthesia or nitrous oxide, lasers, cutting, and the intraoral high-speed handpiece.
- The certified procedures in 0460-04-.08(2) — coronal polishing, nitrous monitoring, sealants, expanded restorative/prosthetic functions, and exposing radiographs — because those require registration plus Board certification.
- "It is the intent of this rule that practical dental assistants not invade the practice procedures only allowed to be assigned or delegated to registered dental assistants or licensed dental hygienists." (0460-04-.01(1)(b)2.)

## Cautions
- Direct supervision always. (0460-04-.01(1)(b)1.)
- Discipline exposure: T.C.A. § 63-5-116(b). (0460-04-.08(5))

**Open.** None.
