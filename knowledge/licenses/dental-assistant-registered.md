# Registered dental assistant — what Tennessee allows

**Rule.** A registered dental assistant holds a Board registration under 0460-04-.02 and, under the direct supervision of a dentist, performs delegated duties plus the extra procedures for which the Board has certified them (0460-04-.01(2); 0460-04-.08(2)-(3)).

## Allowed with Board certification — direct supervision — 0460-04-.08(2)
| Task | Certification rule | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Coronal polishing | 0460-04-.04 | 0460-04-.08(2)(a) |
| Monitoring nitrous oxide | 0460-04-.05 | (2)(b) |
| Sealants | 0460-04-.09 | (2)(c) |
| Expanded restorative functions | 0460-04-.10 | (2)(d) |
| Expanded prosthetic functions | 0460-04-.10 | (2)(e) |
| Exposing dental radiographs | 0460-04-.11 | (2)(f) |

## Allowed when delegated (practical or registered assistant) — 0460-04-.08(3)
The dentist delegates only what the assistant is adequately trained for, under the dentist's direct supervision and full responsibility (0460-04-.08(1)).
| Task | Cite |
|---|---|
| Process (not expose) radiographs, including digital | (3)(a) |
| Topical fluoride; diet instruction; oral hygiene demonstration | (3)(b),(c),(kk) |
| Blood pressure, pulse, temperature, medical history; chart oral conditions | (3)(d) |
| Instrument and operatory infection control; tray setup | (3)(e),(f) |
| Matrices; remove cement from restorations and bands; remove sutures and staples | (3)(g)-(i) |
| Temporary restorations; rubber dam; socket and periodontal dressings | (3)(j)-(m) |
| Plaque smears; oral cytologic smears; alginate impressions not for permanent restorations | (3)(n),(o),(gg) |
| Orthodontic tasks: remove ligature/arch wires; bend/select/place arch wires after dentist approval; bands/brackets; separators; springs, hooks, chain elastics, ligation | (3)(p)-(x) |
| Retraction cord; remove excess supragingival cement by hand instruments only | (3)(y),(z) |
| Place amalgam for condensation by the dentist; cavity bases and liners; tooth conditioners | (3)(aa),(dd),(ee) |
| Topical anesthetics; desensitizing agents | (3)(bb),(cc) |
| Select and pre-fit stainless steel or preformed crowns for the dentist to insert | (3)(ff) |
| Pulp testing; pack pulpotomy paste; dry canals with paper points | (3)(hh)-(jj) |
| Call in prescriptions to the pharmacist as instructed by the dentist | (3)(ll) |
| Fit, adjust and cement correctional appliances; wound care; irrigate extraction site; exposure chains and attachments | (3)(mm)-(pp) |

## Not allowed — 0460-04-.08(4)
Examination, diagnosis and treatment planning; surgical or cutting procedures on hard or soft tissue, including laser, air abrasion, micro-abrasion, curettage or root planing; fitting, adjusting and placing prosthodontic appliances; issuing prescriptions or unauthorized medications, or work authorizations; direct pulp capping, pulpotomy and other unauthorized endodontic procedures; approving the final occlusion; placing sutures; administering local anesthesia, nitrous oxide, conscious sedation or general anesthesia; monitoring nitrous oxide, coronal polishing, sealants, exposing radiographs, or expanded restorative/prosthetic functions without the matching certification; intraoral high-speed handpiece; laser equipment.

## Cautions
- Lasers: "Only dentists licensed by the Tennessee Board of Dentistry shall be authorized to perform procedures involving laser technology." (0460-04-.08(4)(m))
- Nitrous oxide: an assistant may only monitor (with certification), never administer. (4)(h)-(i)
- Excess cement: supragingival surfaces, hand instruments only. (3)(z)
- Out-of-scope work, work without direct supervision, or uncertified polishing, sealants or nitrous monitoring may lead to discipline under T.C.A. § 63-5-116(b). (5)

**Open.** Certification requirements (course hours, exams) live in 0460-04-.04, .05, .09, .10 and .11 — see those cards.
