# Licensed dental hygienist — what Tennessee allows

**Rule.** A licensed dental hygienist performs duties the employer dentist assigns or delegates under Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-03-.09(1); some duties need direct supervision and a Board certification; listed procedures are prohibited (0460-03-.09(7)).

## Allowed (delegated by the employer dentist) — 0460-03-.09(1)
| Task | Supervision / condition | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Remove hard and soft deposits and stains to the depth of the gingival sulcus; polish; clinical exam of teeth and tissues for the dentist's diagnosis | Delegated by employer dentist | 0460-03-.09(1)(a) |
| Prophylaxis | Delegated | (1)(b) |
| Sealants | Delegated | (1)(c) |
| Expose radiographs, including digital | Delegated | (1)(d) |
| Topical fluoride | Delegated | (1)(e) |
| Diet instruction; oral hygiene demonstration | Delegated | (1)(f)-(g) |
| Blood pressure, pulse, temperature, medical history; chart oral conditions | Delegated | (1)(h) |
| Chairside assisting; instrument and operatory infection control; tray setup | Delegated | (1)(i)-(k) |

## Not allowed — 0460-03-.09(7)
Comprehensive examination, diagnosis and treatment planning; surgical or cutting procedures on hard or soft tissue, including laser, air abrasion or micro-abrasion (except curettage or root planing); fitting, adjusting and placing prosthodontic appliances; issuing prescriptions beyond the limited authority in T.C.A. § 63-5-108(h), unauthorized medications, or work authorizations; direct pulp capping, pulpotomy and other unauthorized endodontic procedures; approving the final occlusion; placing sutures; conscious sedation or general anesthesia; local anesthesia without 0460-03-.12 certification; nitrous oxide without 0460-03-.06 certification; intraoral high-speed handpiece.

## Cautions
- Supervision: the dentist may be absent (general supervision) only under T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5) — at least 1 year full-time experience, dentist examined the patient within 11 months and authorized the procedures, at most 15 consecutive business days, and no more than 3 hygienists under general supervision at once (§ 63-5-108(f)(1)); root planing, curettage, nitrous oxide, local anesthesia and restorative/prosthetic functions always need direct supervision. See card tn-63-5-115-supervision-definitions.
- Coming change: starting January 1, 2027, 2026 Pub. Ch. 1107 lets a hygienist take diagnostic radiographs, record tissue data, perform prophylaxis and apply fluoride before the dentist sees the patient, and widens prescriptive authority (for example, silver diamine fluoride). See card tn-0460-03-hygienist-scope-summary.
- Local anesthesia: the dentist must be physically present at the same office location at that time. (5)
- Lasers: examination/periodontal use only; cutting with lasers is prohibited. (1)(ww), (7)(b)
- Working beyond these limits or without required supervision/certification may lead to discipline under T.C.A. § 63-5-116. (8)

**Exact text.**
> "Administration of local anesthesia must be under the direct supervision of a licensed dentist who, at that time, is physically present at the same office location." (0460-03-.09(5)).
> "Use of a high-speed handpiece intraorally" (listed as prohibited, 0460-03-.09(7)(k)).

**Open.** None.
